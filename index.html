01

What is a black hole?

A black hole is a region of space where gravity pulls so hard that nothing can escape it. It forms when a huge amount of matter is crushed into an incredibly small space. Not even light — the fastest thing in existence — can break free once it falls too close.

02

The point of no return

The event horizon is the edge of a black hole. Cross it, and there is no path back out — every direction leads inward. It is the boundary of a region the universe hides from us completely.

03

A disk of fire

Gas and dust falling toward a black hole spiral into a flat, blazing disk. Friction heats this matter to millions of degrees until it glows. The black hole's gravity then bends that light, wrapping the disk into a halo around the dark.

04 · The experiment

How strong is a black hole?

Strong enough to trap light itself. Fire a beam past one and watch what happens — aim too close, and it can never leave. See it for yourself.
